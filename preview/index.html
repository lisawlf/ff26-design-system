Forward 2026

The visual system behind #FF26, derived from the Launch Day Announcement deck. Four typefaces, one accent colour, a fixed set of components and a canvas per export format. Everything on this page is live CSS from css/ff26.css — what you see here is what ships.

Colour

One accent, one ink, a short greyscale. Acid is a spotlight, not a surface: exactly one acid element per composition. Backgrounds are off-white (#FAFAFA), never pure white — pure white is reserved for cards sitting on photographs.

Typography

Four families, four jobs. Sora shouts, Noto Serif Display (extra-condensed, light) adds the one human line, Barlow explains, JetBrains Mono states facts. A slide that uses all four in that order is on-brand almost automatically.

Templates

Eighteen ready-to-fill layouts. Templates 01–11 recreate the launch deck slide for slide; 12–16 extend the same parts to speakers, quotes, stories, squares and the LinkedIn cover; 17–18 add the partner wall and the navy earth look. Open one, replace the copy, export. Each tile links to its file.

Rules

Do

  • One acid element per canvas — highlight, button, table header or a single bar.
  • Set headlines uppercase in Sora and let them run to three short lines.
  • Follow every display headline with exactly one serif lede line.
  • Put every number in JetBrains Mono. That is what makes claims read as facts.
  • Scrim photographs before placing white text, or knock the text out of a solid bar.
  • Keep the date/venue meta bar on the first and last slide of every carousel.

Don't

  • Don't use acid as a page background or for body copy — it stops being a signal.
  • Don't introduce a fifth typeface, a gradient, or a drop shadow.
  • Don't set the serif in uppercase, or the display in sentence case.
  • Don't put white text straight onto an unprotected photograph.
  • Don't stretch, recolour or box the logo. Black or white, nothing else.
  • Don't use more than one sticker in a carousel — it is a wink, not a voice.
